## New Indonesian paid-social copy

### Supplied brief

- Product: RapiKas matches incoming bank transfers with invoices and flags unmatched payments.
- Audience: finance teams at Indonesian service businesses.
- Verified terms: 14-day trial, no card required, CSV export.
- Unknown: time saved, customer count, accuracy rate, and testimonial.
- Desired action: start a trial.

### Recommended copy

> Pembayaran masuk, tapi invoice yang lunas masih harus dicari satu-satu?
>
> RapiKas mencocokkan transfer bank dengan invoice dan menandai transaksi yang masih perlu dicek. Hasilnya bisa diekspor ke CSV.
>
> Coba 14 hari tanpa kartu.

Headline:

> Cocokkan transfer dengan invoice

CTA:

> Coba 14 hari

### Why it is publishable

- The opening names a recognizable job rather than claiming a universal pain.
- The mechanism carries the value.
- The trial and card term come from the supplied brief.
- The draft does not invent speed, accuracy, popularity, or savings.

Do not automatically include this reasoning when the user asks for copy only.

## New English B2B copy

### Supplied brief

- Product: LedgerLane connects Stripe payouts to invoice records and flags mismatches.
- Audience: US finance operations teams.
- Verified proof: supports Stripe and NetSuite; exports an audit log.
- Unknown: conversion lift, hours saved, and customer logos.
- Desired action: book a live demo.

### Landing-page hero

> **Find payout mismatches before month-end**
>
> LedgerLane connects Stripe payouts to NetSuite invoices, flags records that need review, and exports the audit trail.
>
> **See a live demo**

Support line near CTA:

> Bring one payout report. We’ll show how it maps in your workflow.

### Avoid

> Revolutionize month-end with the world’s most seamless reconciliation platform.

The avoided line adds ranking and quality claims without proof and hides the product mechanism.

## Emoji decision

### Supplied brief

- Product: a bakery’s weekend cinnamon-roll batch.
- Verified offer: orders close Friday at 17.00 or earlier if 80 boxes sell.
- Brand voice: warm, concise, usually one food emoji at the end.
- Channel: Instagram.

### Fitting use

> Cinnamon roll dipanggang Sabtu pagi dan dikirim di hari yang sama. Pesanan tutup Jumat pukul 17.00 atau saat 80 boks habis.
>
> Pilih jumlah boks 🍞

The emoji reinforces the food context after a complete instruction. The actual deadline and stock rule remain in words.

### Poor use

> 🔥 BURUAN!!! Tinggal dikit 😱 Klik sekarang 👉👉👉

The poor version obscures the terms, adds fear pressure, and replaces information with decorative signals.

## Service recovery

### Customer message

> Pesanan saya dijanjikan datang kemarin, tapi sampai sekarang belum ada. Ini untuk acara kantor siang ini.

### Response

> Maaf, pesanan Anda belum tiba sesuai jadwal. Saya sudah cek nomor kirim RK1842: paket masih berada di gudang Depok dan tidak akan sampai sebelum acara siang ini.
>
> Saya bisa membatalkan pesanan dan memproses pengembalian dana hari ini, atau mengirim ulang untuk besok tanpa biaya. Mana yang Anda pilih?

Why no emoji: the situation is negative, time-sensitive, and requires competence. The response acknowledges the failure, gives a concrete status, presents available remedies, and lets the customer choose.

## Transcreation

### Source facts and English copy

- Product action: turns a meeting transcript into assigned follow-up items.
- Limitation: a person must confirm every assignment.

> **Leave the call with owners, not loose ends.**
>
> Tindak turns your transcript into a draft follow-up list. Review each owner, then send it to the team.

### Weak literal Indonesian

> **Tinggalkan panggilan dengan pemilik, bukan ujung yang longgar.**
>
> Tindak mengubah transkrip Anda menjadi daftar tindak lanjut draf.

### Transcreated Indonesian

> **Selesai rapat, tiap tindak lanjut sudah punya penanggung jawab.**
>
> Tindak menyusun draf tugas dari transkrip rapat. Periksa nama penanggung jawabnya, lalu kirim ke tim.

The target preserves the outcome, mechanism, review requirement, and action while rebuilding an English idiom.

## Copy audit

### Draft

> Di era bisnis yang dinamis, NovaHR hadir sebagai solusi revolusioner untuk memberdayakan tim Anda, meningkatkan produktivitas, dan menciptakan pengalaman karyawan yang seamless. 🚀

### Compact audit

- `Di era bisnis yang dinamis` — generic scene-setting; it delays the customer situation. Delete it.
- `solusi revolusioner` — unsupported quality claim. Replace it with the product action.
- `memberdayakan`, `meningkatkan`, `menciptakan` — three abstract benefits with no mechanism or proof.
- `seamless` — prestige English without a defined experience.
- `🚀` — decorative and inconsistent with an unproven enterprise claim.

### Rewrite when the only verified fact is “employees submit leave and managers approve it in one dashboard”

> Karyawan mengajukan cuti, manajer memeriksa jadwal tim, lalu menyetujui permintaan dari satu dashboard.

Do not add `lebih cepat`, `tanpa repot`, or an adoption figure until evidence supports it.

## Test variants

### Supplied brief

- Product: the verified RapiKas offer from the first example.
- Control: the current generic ad says `Kelola invoice lebih mudah dengan RapiKas`.
- Business metric: completed trials that connect a bank account, not CTR alone.

### Control

> Kelola invoice lebih mudah dengan RapiKas.

### Variant A: problem recognition

> Transfer sudah masuk, tapi invoice lunas masih dicari satu-satu? RapiKas mencocokkannya dan menandai transaksi yang perlu dicek. Coba 14 hari tanpa kartu.

Hypothesis: a recognizable reconciliation moment will increase qualified trial activation over the generic benefit.

### Variant B: mechanism first

> Hubungkan transfer bank dengan invoice. RapiKas mencocokkan pembayaran, menandai selisih, dan mengekspor hasilnya ke CSV. Coba 14 hari tanpa kartu.

Hypothesis: a concrete mechanism will increase qualified trial activation among problem-aware finance teams.

Do not add a third version that merely swaps synonyms. Keep audience, offer, placement, and creative stable enough to interpret the result.
