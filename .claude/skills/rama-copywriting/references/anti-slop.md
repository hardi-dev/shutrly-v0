# Anti-slop editing

Use this as an editorial lint, not an AI detector. No word, punctuation mark, sentence shape, or detector score proves authorship. Diagnose clusters of generic, abstract, over-patterned writing that fail to sound like this brand speaking to this customer.

## The standard

Human-sounding copy is not deliberately messy. It is situated: a particular speaker notices a particular reader’s situation, makes a bounded claim, uses evidence they actually have, and chooses language natural to that relationship.

Do not add typos, random slang, invented anecdotes, confessions, filler, or broken grammar to seem human. Do not sand every line into the same minimalist style either.

Ask the strongest diagnostic question:

> Could a competitor paste this line into its ad unchanged?

If yes, add the missing customer situation, mechanism, proof, trade-off, or voice—or delete the line.

## Voice fingerprint

Before editing, compare the draft with real approved samples:

- Does it use the same pronouns and level of distance?
- Are sentence lengths and paragraph breaks in the observed range?
- Does it repeat the brand’s actual product terms rather than cycle synonyms?
- Is its humor dry, playful, sharp, warm, or absent in the same way?
- Does it use punctuation, contractions, code-switching, and emoji similarly?
- Does it make the same kinds of claims and acknowledge limits the same way?

Match behaviors, not an adjective such as `bold`, `human`, or `friendly`. If asked to clone a living writer’s exact style, extract high-level traits and create an original voice instead of close imitation.

## Pattern scan

Treat every item as a signal to inspect in context, not a ban.

### Chatbot residue

- `Great question!`, `Of course!`, `Certainly!`
- `Tentu!`, `Berikut adalah...`
- `Let’s dive in`, `Mari kita bahas`, `Here’s what you need to know`
- `I hope this helps`, `Let me know if...`

Delete correspondence that accidentally became ad copy.

### Generic scene-setting

- `In today’s fast-paced world...`
- `Di era digital yang serba cepat...`
- broad claims about a changing landscape before the customer appears
- a heading followed by a sentence that merely repeats the heading

Start where the customer’s actual situation starts.

### Inflated significance

- `revolutionary`, `game-changing`, `pivotal`, `transformative`, `groundbreaking`
- `inovatif`, `terdepan`, `solusi terbaik`, `membawa ke level berikutnya`
- `a testament to`, `marks a new era`, `menjadi bukti nyata`

Replace ceremonial importance with an observable change and its evidence.

### Abstract promotional stacks

- `seamless, powerful, intuitive`
- `aman, mudah, dan terpercaya`
- `efficiency, productivity, and growth`
- `efisien, optimal, dan maksimal`
- strings of nominalizations, benefits, or adjectives without an actor or mechanism

Keep the one property that matters, then show how the product produces it.

### Empty authority and proof

- `experts say`, `industry-leading`, `studies show`, `dipercaya banyak orang`
- unnamed awards, customers, research, ratings, or “data”
- exact-looking numbers with no supplied source
- a testimonial polished beyond the customer’s actual words

Name and link the source, qualify it accurately, or remove the appeal.

### Fake intimacy and empathy

- `We know exactly how you feel.`
- `Kami mengerti semua perjuangan Anda.`
- unearned `bestie`, `sobat`, `Bunda`, `Bos`, or `Kak`
- guessed identity, emotion, or private behavior

Show understanding through a precise situation or useful response. Do not claim emotional access you do not have.

### Formula fingerprints

- repeated `not just X, but Y` or `bukan sekadar X, tetapi Y`
- repeated `from X to Y` pairs that are not a real range
- `whether you’re X, Y, or Z`
- forced groups of three
- question after question as a hook
- every paragraph ending in a slogan
- every line using the same short-fragment rhythm
- repeated em dashes, colons, bold labels, or emoji bullets

Break the mold only where the meaning improves. A deliberate parallel structure can still be excellent copy.

### Synthetic enthusiasm

- all caps, stacked exclamation marks, fire/rocket/sparkle sequences
- generic superlatives and `100%` without proof
- a triumphant ending that adds no term, proof, or action
- `Don’t miss out`, `Jangan lewatkan`, or `Act now` without genuine reason

Use the intensity earned by the offer and relationship.

### False oppositions and fake depth

- `The real question is...`, `At its core...`, `What really matters...`
- `Ini bukan soal X. Ini soal Y.` when X and Y are not meaningfully opposed
- trailing `-ing` or Indonesian subordinate phrases that pretend to explain impact without doing so

State the point and consequence directly.

### Thesaurus variation

Do not rotate through `customer`, `buyer`, `shopper`, `consumer`, and `user` merely to avoid repetition. Reuse the correct term. The same applies to Indonesian synonym cycling.

### Generic conclusions

- `The future is bright.`
- `Saatnya memulai perjalanan menuju kesuksesan.`
- `Exciting things are ahead.`

End with the next step, a decision-relevant term, or nothing.

## Five editing passes

### 1. Truth and proof

Mark every express and implied claim. Remove or flag any number, comparison, outcome, urgency, review, guarantee, or authority without supplied evidence. Verify that qualifications do not contradict the headline’s net impression.

### 2. Specificity and mechanism

Underline adjectives, abstractions, and broad benefits. Replace each with one of these when available:

- the object or action;
- a real customer moment;
- how the feature works;
- observable proof;
- a relevant limitation.

Do not manufacture detail to pass this check.

### 3. Voice and language

Compare the copy with the voice fingerprint. Fix pronoun drift, formality shifts, translation-shaped phrasing, prestige English, random slang, and inconsistent terminology.

### 4. Rhythm and structure

Read the copy aloud. Combine fragments that sound staged. Split clauses that bury the point. Remove repeated openings, forced triples, symmetrical slogans, and decorative transitions. Keep purposeful repetition.

### 5. Compression

Delete every line that does not add relevance, value, mechanism, proof, risk handling, or next step. Then restore any personality or context the compression removed. Shorter is not automatically better; denser and clearer is.

## Before and after

All product facts below are illustrative; publish them only if verified.

### Indonesian

Before:

> Di era digital yang serba cepat, RamaFlow hadir sebagai solusi inovatif yang dirancang khusus untuk membantu bisnis Anda bekerja lebih efisien, produktif, dan optimal. 🚀

After:

> Invoice numpuk tiap Jumat? RamaFlow mencocokkan pembayaran bank dengan invoice dan menandai transaksi yang masih perlu dicek.

The revision removes a generic era, empty adjectives, forced triple, and decorative emoji. It adds a customer moment and mechanism.

### English

Before:

> Unlock seamless productivity with our revolutionary platform—designed to empower your team and elevate every workflow. 🚀✨

After:

> Close the books without chasing spreadsheets. RamaFlow matches bank payments to invoices and flags the ones that need a person.

The revision names the work, mechanism, and exception instead of claiming a universal transformation.

### Unsupported proof

Before:

> Dipercaya ribuan bisnis di Indonesia.

If the figure and scope are verified:

> 1.842 toko aktif memproses pesanan dengan RamaFlow pada Juni 2026.

If they are not verified: delete the sentence. Never replace one unsupported claim with a more precise invention.

## Final scorecard

Score each item `0` or `1`. Revise any zero before publishing.

- **Particular:** a competitor cannot reuse the central message unchanged.
- **Concrete:** the reader can picture the situation, action, or mechanism.
- **Proven:** material claims are supported or honestly qualified.
- **Natural:** a speaker using this register could say it aloud without strain.
- **Consistent:** pronouns, terminology, locale, tone, and punctuation stay stable.
- **Varied:** rhythm serves meaning instead of repeating a model-shaped pattern.
- **Useful:** every line helps recognition, belief, choice, or action.
- **Original:** no close imitation, copied swipe, or polished fake testimonial.
- **Accessible:** meaning survives without formatting, color, or emoji.
- **Clean:** no chatbot residue, generic ceremony, or invented personality remains.
